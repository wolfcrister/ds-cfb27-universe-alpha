- [ ] Confirm the total number of eligible programs in CFB 27.
- [ ] Record the final preseason Top 25.
- [ ] Record OVR for every program.
- [ ] Record offensive and defensive OVR where available.
- [ ] Record prestige for use only as a late tiebreaker.
- [ ] Confirm every program's city and state.
- [ ] Add coordinates where geographic calculations are required.
- [ ] Replace Kennesaw State with FedEx University in the source list.
- [ ] Preserve screenshots or other direct source evidence.

## 2. Seed Tier 1

- [ ] Add all 25 ranked programs.
- [ ] Remove those teams from the OVR pool.
- [ ] Add the seven highest-OVR unranked programs.
- [ ] Resolve and document any tie at the seventh additional place.
- [ ] Verify exactly 32 Tier 1 teams.

## 3. Seed Tier 3

- [ ] Identify the six lowest-OVR non-Tier-1 programs.
- [ ] Resolve and document any tie at the boundary.
- [ ] Confirm how the FedEx-for-Kennesaw replacement affects the bottom six.
- [ ] Verify exactly six Tier 3 Independents.

## 4. Seed Tier 2

- [ ] Assign every remaining program to Tier 2.
- [ ] Verify exactly 96 Tier 2 teams under the inherited 134-team model.
- [ ] Stop and document any program-count discrepancy.

## 5. Build Tier 1 conferences

- [ ] Compare the 32 teams against the Los Angeles and New York anchors.
- [ ] Create a strict western and eastern split.
- [ ] Resolve only geographic border cases until the split is 16/16.
- [ ] Record every border decision.
- [ ] Sort each conference by latitude.
- [ ] Assign eight northernmost teams to North.
- [ ] Assign eight southernmost teams to South.
- [ ] Verify four divisions of eight.

## 6. Build Tier 2 conferences

- [ ] Divide Tier 2 into four macro-regions of 24.
- [ ] Split West into Pac-12 and Mountain West, 12 each.
- [ ] Split Central into Conference USA and AAC, 12 each.
- [ ] Split South into Sun Belt and SEC, 12 each.
- [ ] Split East/North into MAC and ACC, 12 each.
- [ ] Record geographic border decisions.
- [ ] Perform no strength balancing.

## 7. Configure the game

- [ ] Tier 1 conferences contain 16 teams each.
- [ ] Tier 1 divisions are enabled.
- [ ] Tier 1 divisions contain eight teams each.
- [ ] Tier 1 championship games use division winners.
- [ ] Tier 2 conferences contain 12 teams each.
- [ ] Tier 2 divisions are disabled.
- [ ] Tier 2 championship games use the top two teams.
- [ ] Nine conference games are configured where possible.
- [ ] Protected rivals are configured where possible.
- [ ] Tier 3 contains six Independents.

## 8. Final validation

- [ ] Every eligible program appears exactly once.
- [ ] No program is duplicated.
- [ ] No program is missing.
- [ ] Tier counts equal 32, 96 and 6.
- [ ] Conference counts equal 16 or 12 as required.
- [ ] Qualification and geography are kept separate.
- [ ] All ties and border judgments are documented.
- [ ] `world/alignments/current.yaml` is marked validated.
- [ ] `canon/current-state.yaml` points to the completed alignment.
- [ ] The alignment is committed before the dynasty advances.
