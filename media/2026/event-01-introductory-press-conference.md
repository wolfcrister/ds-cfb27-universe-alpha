# Event 01: Daniel Sandberg Introductory Press Conference

## Status

Confirmed media canon.

## Setting

FedEx University formally introduces Daniel Sandberg as the inaugural head coach of the Couriers in Memphis.

## Central themes

- Sandberg presents the role as the construction of an institution, not merely a coaching appointment.
- FedEx University's first competitive objective is promotion from Tier 3 in its inaugural 2026 season.
- The program will be built through high-school recruiting, junior-college recruiting and the transfer portal.
- High-school recruiting is the long-term foundation. The portal is intended for fit, opportunity and leadership rather than simply purchasing a roster.
- The offensive identity is an adaptable Air Raid with a meaningful running game and receiving role for running backs.
- Line play is described as the foundation of the program.
- Sandberg intends to operate as a delegating head coach, closer to a chief executive than a coordinator attempting to control every detail.
- His first major football task is to hire a head scout and build the scouting department.

## Sandberg on culture

Sandberg connects the program to the FedEx Purple Promise: every experience should be outstanding.

He argues that elite resources do not guarantee achievement. They create an obligation to build and perform at an elite standard.

His leadership model emphasizes:

- honesty rather than false promises
- trust that must be earned
- open disagreement and well-reasoned challenges
- final accountability resting with the head coach
- second chances without tolerating weak links or destructive personalities
- respect for players as developing adults

He rejects blind loyalty, but also rejects taking institutional support for granted.

## Memphis and institutional identity

Sandberg describes FedEx as part of Memphis culture and treats the University of Memphis with respect. He argues that competition can strengthen football in the city.

He also rejects the idea that FedEx University is uniquely unfair. College football already contains major inequalities in wealth, facilities and institutional power. FedEx is simply unusually explicit about its resources.

The university's educational and corporate career pathways are presented as opportunities rather than coercive commitments. Sandberg states that he is the football coach, not the headmaster.

## Public promises

Sandberg does not promise championships. He promises exceptional resources, development and opportunity.

When pressed on how quickly a title could arrive, he jokes that it may take six years.

## Immediate media impression

The press conference establishes Sandberg as candid, self-confident, unconventional and less polished than a traditional corporate spokesperson. Supporters see an intelligent founder. Critics see a first-time head coach fronting an unprecedented corporate experiment.
