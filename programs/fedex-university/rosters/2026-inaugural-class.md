# 2026 FedEx University Inaugural Player Class

## Status

Confirmed high-level roster canon.

The transfer portal and primary recruiting cycle are complete. FedEx University enters National Signing Day with an inaugural class of 60 scholarship players.

## Class construction

- Total incoming players: 60
- Scholarship status: all 60 receive full athletic scholarships
- Primary talent tier: three-star prospects
- Upper tier: several four-star recruits
- Developmental tier: a smaller number of two-star recruits
- Sources: high school, junior college and transfer portal
- Remaining roster capacity: open for later additions within the applicable roster limit

The exact generated roster, player names, positions, ratings and jersey numbers remain subject to the in-game roster.

## Roster-building philosophy

The class is not intended to resemble a normal annual recruiting intake. FedEx University had no inherited roster and therefore had to create a functional team in one cycle.

The staff used three main sources:

1. High-school recruits who can become the long-term identity of the program
2. Junior-college players capable of providing immediate physical maturity
3. Transfers seeking playing time and defined roles

The class is deliberately concentrated around three-star talent rather than an implausible collection of national blue-chip recruits.

## Narrative meaning

The 60-player class changes the public evaluation of FedEx University.

Before signing day, the program was primarily treated as a corporate experiment with elite facilities and a recognizable coach.

After signing day, it possesses enough talent to create expectations of immediate competitiveness and possible promotion from Tier 3.

The roster's central questions are now cohesion, leadership, development and how quickly players from many different backgrounds can become one team.

## Documentation rule

Most of the 60 generated players will be documented through seasonal roster lists, statistics and game reports only.

Individual player profiles should be reserved for players whose recruitment, personality, achievements or major events make them historically significant to the program.
