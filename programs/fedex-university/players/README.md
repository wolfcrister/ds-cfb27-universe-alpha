# FedEx University Player Profiles

## Purpose

This directory stores detailed profiles for players whose recruitment, career, personality or historical significance becomes part of the FedEx University narrative.

Most players on generated in-game rosters do not require individual files. They may remain documented only in seasonal roster tables, statistics or game reports.

A dedicated profile should be created when a player becomes notable because of one or more of the following:

- firsts in program history
- major recruiting or transfer announcements
- unusual personal or family connections
- captaincy or leadership
- important records or awards
- major games or defining moments
- transfer, injury, controversy or redemption storylines
- long-term importance to the program's identity

## Profile convention

Use one Markdown file per notable player:

`firstname-lastname.md`

Profiles should separate confirmed canon from details that remain open. Suggested sections:

1. Quick profile
2. Recruitment or arrival
3. Player identity and projected role
4. Personal background
5. Historical significance
6. Career timeline
7. Canon status
