## Background

Miles Kennedy was a highly regarded four-star quarterback from Memphis who signed with Georgia after receiving offers from major programs across the South.

He spent three seasons in Athens:

- redshirted as a freshman
- appeared in limited late-game action
- competed seriously for the backup role
- developed in an elite SEC environment
- entered the transfer portal after realizing that another season might pass without meaningful playing time

Kennedy did not leave because he failed to develop. He left because development without game experience was no longer enough.

## Player identity

Kennedy is an improvisational quarterback who is comfortable operating both inside and outside structure.

Strengths:

- accurate throwing while moving
- ability to escape pressure without immediately abandoning the pass
- confidence attacking the middle of the field
- pre-snap recognition
- arm strength to reach every area of the field
- ability to keep broken plays alive

Development areas:

- can hold the ball too long searching for a larger play
- occasionally trusts his arm into dangerous windows
- has limited meaningful college game experience
- can turn manageable situations into unnecessary chaos

He is talented enough to become the best player on the inaugural roster and unpredictable enough to become its most entertaining.

## Why FedEx University

Kennedy initially viewed FedEx University as an interesting local spectacle rather than a serious football option. Meetings with Daniel Sandberg and Graham Harrell changed that view.

The pitch was direct:

- return home to Memphis
- receive elite resources
- learn under two former professional players
- operate an offense built around quarterback freedom
- compete to start immediately
- become the first quarterback in school history

Kennedy believes in the program, but playing time is central to the decision. At Georgia, he might never become the starter. At FedEx University, the offense can be built around him.

His commitment statement:

> I did not enter the portal because I wanted an easier road. I entered because I wanted the chance to actually lead a team. There is only one place where I can be the first quarterback in school history, and it happens to be home.

Daniel Sandberg's public response:

> Miles is not coming here to lend us credibility. He is coming here to play quarterback and win football games.

Graham Harrell's evaluation:

> There are quarterbacks who can execute a play, and there are quarterbacks who can keep the offense alive when the play stops existing. Miles can do both.

## Name note

Kennedy has spent much of his life explaining that he shares his name with rock singer Myles Kennedy, although the spelling differs.

## Historical significance

Kennedy is:

- the first major transfer quarterback in program history
- the first likely starting quarterback of the Couriers
- the first nationally significant player addition
- the early public face of the inaugural roster
