## Arrival

DeShawn Booker arrives from East Mississippi Community College as the most physically ready defensive lineman in the inaugural FedEx University class.

He originally drew FBS interest out of high school but took the JUCO route after academic issues limited his options. Two productive seasons rebuilt both his transcript and his recruiting standing.

Several established programs viewed Booker as rotational depth. FedEx University offered him a more direct role: become the player around whom the first defensive front is built.

## Player identity

Booker is a powerful interior defender with the size and strength to control the line of scrimmage immediately.

Strengths:

- heavy, violent hands
- excellent lower-body strength
- ability to absorb double teams
- disruptive bull rush
- short-area quickness for his size
- physical readiness for immediate college snaps

He is expected to anchor the run defense and provide interior pressure from the opening game.

## Why FedEx University

Booker was drawn to the honesty of the pitch. The staff did not promise vague opportunity. It told him that the new defense needed a proven adult presence in the middle.

His arrival statement:

> They didn't tell me I might play. They told me they needed someone to build the defensive line around.

## Historical significance

Booker is:

- the first major JUCO addition in program history
- the first veteran defensive building block
- potentially one of the highest-rated players on the inaugural roster
- an early candidate to become the physical identity of the first Couriers defense
