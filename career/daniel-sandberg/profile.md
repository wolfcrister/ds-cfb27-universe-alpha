# Daniel Sandberg

## Current role

Daniel Sandberg is the inaugural head coach of the FedEx University Couriers.

- Program: FedEx University
- Location: Memphis, Tennessee
- Role: Head coach
- Tenure begins: 2027 season
- Program tier: Tier 3
- Offensive identity: Air Raid

## Background

Sandberg is a former player recruited to establish the football identity of a completely new university and athletic program.

FedEx University can provide elite facilities, staffing resources, recruiting infrastructure and financial backing. It cannot provide inherited tradition, veteran roster continuity or an established football culture. Sandberg has been hired to build those things from the beginning.

## Initial coaching challenge

The inaugural roster has no returning FedEx players. It must be assembled through:

- Freshman recruiting
- Junior-college recruiting
- The transfer portal

The intended roster identity is talented but structurally incomplete. A veteran transfer quarterback, an established wide receiver and a leading defensive lineman may provide immediate star quality, while the offensive line, defensive rotation and overall depth remain developmental.

## Initial competitive expectation

A 7-5 or 8-4 season is the intended competitive target if the team is managed well. This is a design expectation, not a guaranteed canonical result.
